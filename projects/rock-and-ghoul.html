Overview

Rock & Ghoul is a 2D action game developed by Team Avid Divas. You play as Rock, a guitarist who fights off spirits with her guitar and elemental abilities while trying to reach the stage and reunite with Ghoul.

Our team built the game using a custom C++ engine. My main responsibility was graphics programming, working on the systems that control how the game renders its scenes and visual elements.

Gameplay

The game includes movement, dashing, melee attacks, and spells. The short recording of the finished game is displayed above.

My contributions

I was the Graphics Champion Programmer. My work focused mainly on textures, render layers, cameras, and shaders.

  • Textures: Worked on texture handling for the game's rendered assets.
  • Render layers: Worked on how visual elements are organized and rendered in the correct layers.
  • Cameras: Worked on camera functionality used to display the game world.
  • Shaders: Worked with graphics shaders in the OpenGL rendering pipeline.

These systems needed to work alongside gameplay, UI, and the team's other engine components, so testing and debugging across systems was an important part of the process.

Custom engine & tools

Our team developed a custom engine with an ImGui-based editor for working with scenes and game entities. The editor provided tools for viewing render layers, adjusting scene content, and testing gameplay.

The engine supported separate Background, GameObject, Foreground, and UI render layers. It also included scene management and debugging tools that helped the team develop and test the game.

What I learned

Rock & Ghoul gave me experience working on graphics systems in a larger C++ project. I learned more about how rendering components fit together, how to debug visual issues, and how to coordinate changes with teammates working on other parts of the engine.

This was a team project. The sections above focus on my graphics responsibilities; the game engine and finished game were developed collaboratively by Team Avid Divas.
