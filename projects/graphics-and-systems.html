Overview

I worked on a series of graphics projects using C++, OpenGL, and GLSL. They focus on how real-time images are produced, from writing shaders to applying lighting, textures, and rendering effects.

I enjoyed seeing how small changes to shader code could make a noticeable difference to a scene, and learning to debug results that were often easier to see than to explain.

Rendering work

  • Environment mapping: experimented with reflections and environment-based effects in rendered scenes.
  • Sky and scene rendering: worked on a night-sky rendering.
  • Graphics filters: explored shader-based image effects and post-processing.
  • Portal effects: worked on a portal-style graphics involving visual rendering effects.

What I learned

The projects helped me get more comfortable with the rendering pipeline and the relationship between C++ application code, GPU resources, and GLSL shaders. They also reinforced the importance of testing one rendering change at a time and checking intermediate results when an effect wasn't behaving as expected.
