Overview

I worked on a series of graphics projects using C++, OpenGL, and GLSL. These projects explored how real-time scenes are rendered, including shaders, lighting, textures, and visual effects.

I particularly enjoyed seeing how changes in shader code could affect the appearance of a scene. Debugging visual issues also helped me understand how different stages of the rendering pipeline work together.

Rendering work

  • Environment mapping: Experimented with reflections and environment-based visual effects.
  • Sky and scene rendering: Worked on rendering a night-sky scene.
  • Graphics filters: Explored shader-based image effects and post-processing.
  • Portal effects: Worked on portal-style visual effects involving shader-based rendering.

What I learned

These projects helped me understand the relationship between C++ application code, GPU resources, and GLSL shaders.

I also learned the importance of testing one rendering change at a time and inspecting intermediate results when an effect wasn't behaving as expected.
